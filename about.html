About This Blog

The Football Corner started as a way to write down thoughts after watching matches instead of just yelling at the screen. It's grown into a small collection of tactical analysis, player spotlights, and match reactions for fans who want more depth than a highlight reel.

About the Author

I'm a student and lifelong football fan who got tired of surface-level takes online. Every article here comes from actually watching the matches and doing the research, not copy-pasting headlines.
